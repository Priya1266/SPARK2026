About SPARK 2026

Four days. Two circuits. One spark of innovation.

SPARK 2026 is a national-level technical summit organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology. The summit brings together student innovators, coders, circuit-builders and technology enthusiasts for technical competitions, innovation and engineering challenges.

⚡ National Level Technical Summit
🏆 Competitive Engineering Challenges
🎓 Student Innovation
🤖 Robotics & Technology
💡 Ideas & Innovation
